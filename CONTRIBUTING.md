# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help. OpenDevTrack is licensed under the [Mozilla Public License 2.0](LICENSE).

## Before the first pull request: the CLA

Contributions are accepted under the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). It leaves you the copyright of your work and gives the project a licence broad enough to distribute it, also under other licence terms in the future (for example a commercial edition next to the open-source one).

To accept it, add this sentence to the description of your first pull request, with your name:

> I have read the OpenDevTrack Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md) and I agree to it for this and my future contributions. — *Your Name*

If you contribute on behalf of your employer, make sure you are allowed to, or ask for an agreement signed by the company.

## How to work

- Read the [project rules](AGENTS.md): conventions, boundaries and the checks to run.
- Everything is in English: code, comments, tests, documentation, commit messages. Italian exists only as a translation catalogue.
- One change per pull request, with its tests and documentation; Conventional Commits for the messages.
- Run the checks of the project rules before opening the pull request; CI repeats them.
- What OpenDevTrack misses in Quadrava or in ms-authz is added there, in their own repositories, not here.
- Report security problems privately, not in a public issue: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

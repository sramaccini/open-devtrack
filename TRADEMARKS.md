## What you may do

- Use the name to refer to the project truthfully, for example «built on OpenDevTrack», «compatible with OpenDevTrack», «a fork of OpenDevTrack».
- Use the logo, unmodified and following the rules in [brand/README.md](brand/README.md), to refer to the project: in articles, talks, documentation, lists of tools, links to the repository.
- Keep the name and the logo in an unmodified copy of the project, or in a copy that only adds packaging or configuration.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name «OpenDevTrack» or with its logo. A modified fork must take a different name and remove or replace the files in `brand/` and the logo shown in the README.
- Using the name or the logo in the name or identity of your own product, company or domain, or in a way suggesting endorsement by the project.
- Changing the logo, or using it as part of another logo.

For permissions, open an issue in the repository.
